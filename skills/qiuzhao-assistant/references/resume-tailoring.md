# Evidence-Bounded Resume Tailoring

Read this reference when the user asks for a resume, profile, self-introduction, project rewrite, or application answer customized to a JD.

## Core rule

Tailoring means selecting, ordering, compressing, and rephrasing verified material. It does not mean inventing a missing experience. Keep the master resume unchanged and create a named variant linked to the target JD.

## Requirement-to-evidence matrix

Create this before drafting:

| JD requirement | Importance | Candidate evidence | Coverage | Safe treatment |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Example requirement | high/medium/low | `experience_id` or `unknown` | strong/partial/missing | emphasize / qualify / omit / ask |

Interpret coverage as follows:

- `strong`: the source clearly shows the capability and the user's scope;
- `partial`: related evidence exists but the exact requirement or level is not confirmed;
- `missing`: do not claim it; ask whether there is additional evidence or mark as a gap.

## Positioning choices

Use the employer and role's actual work chain, not a generic “best resume.” Typical choices:

- technical support/solutions: product understanding, troubleshooting, documentation, communication, and cross-team delivery;
- design/R&D/engineering: methods, models, tools, experiments, validation, and technical reasoning;
- central/SOE or regulated employers: technical foundation, reliability, compliance, organization, and the employer's required formal fields;
- foreign electrical/automation: product context, engineering evidence, English materials when requested, and concise cross-functional communication;
- university/admin/operations: coordination, writing, organization, service, and leadership evidence.

If the user has a preferred positioning rule, record it in the application card for reproducibility.

## Safe rewrite rules

- Preserve dates, titles, degree names, tool names, ownership, and result status.
- Convert vague activity into context → action → verified result, but leave the result qualified when it is not measured.
- Do not convert coursework into production experience.
- Do not convert a team contribution into sole ownership.
- Do not promote a simulation, demo, draft, or proposed architecture into deployed production work.
- Do not add ATS keywords unless the source material supports them; place unmatched JD keywords in a gap list.
- For limited-length fields, prioritize truthful fit and record what was omitted.
- Keep the user's original source paragraph or link so edits can be reversed.

## Deliverables

For each tailored version, produce:

1. the tailored resume or field text;
2. the requirement-to-evidence matrix;
3. a short change summary;
4. unresolved questions and missing evidence;
5. a version identifier and target JD link.
