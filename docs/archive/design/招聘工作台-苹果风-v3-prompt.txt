Preserve the product functions and four-entry navigation, but redesign visual rhythm, surfaces, proportions and logo. This must look genuinely different from a recolor of the input. Core art direction:
Native Mac productivity app quality, inspired by the clarity of Calendar, Reminders and Notes without copying their brand identities. Calm white and neutral mist-gray surfaces. A very subtle frosted sidebar with a faint sage tint, lightly translucent unified top toolbar, precise typography, soft continuous corners and exceptionally careful spacing. Texture should be nearly imperceptible, not grain/noise. Color stays GREEN, but green is a restrained lively jade accent #2C8064 on a mostly white/soft-gray canvas. Main text #202724, secondary #6D7671, separator #EBEEEB. Avoid dark forest corporate slabs, beige/yellow cast, huge filled green regions, cold blue, neon, excessive glass blur or shiny 3D. No visible border around every section. No table-like grid of boxed cards. Use whitespace and native list rhythm. One only soft elevated interview feature surface, not a card farm.

Original logo and wordmark:
Replace the previous chunky interlocking-doorway logo completely. Design a new small elegant ORIGINAL abstract symbol, approx 28-32px, drawn as a single smooth open ascending loop with a clean tapered terminal and a tiny offset circular endpoint. Free-standing, no square tile, no Chinese character, no letter monogram, no corporate building/door/chain/bracket imagery, no heavy bevel, no generic briefcase or handshake, no apple, leaf, robot or sparkle. Do not imitate Apple's logo or any existing company mark. Use a light/medium weight sculpted curve, not two thick angular pieces. Adjacent exact wordmark 招聘工作台 in a medium-weight modern Chinese sans-serif, smaller and less heavy than the reference. This product is for job seekers, not a recruiter CRM.
